Your work, connected

One workspace for your AI and the things you create.

Fusfy brings conversations, a searchable Library, connected calendars and a social content workspace together in a desktop application.

Explore the workflowUnderstand your data

Conversations with context

Use chat or voice with your selected model. Enabled memory sources and connected tools help you find earlier work and continue it.

A Library of your work

Find files and captured outputs from Fusfy and supported connected workflows. Images, sessions and source references keep their origins.

Content, from draft to review

Prepare media and channel-specific posts, then review what will be sent. The local publishing queue runs on your computer.

Connect the services you choose

Google Calendar and mail connections use permissions you approve. The social workspace supports a local YouTube uploader and adapters for other platforms. Direct API availability depends on each platform's application approval and your account permissions.

Social connections are in development. YouTube's application is in testing. TikTok is not approved for public use. LinkedIn and Meta registrations are incomplete. A browser fallback is available where a direct connection is unavailable.

Keep control of publishing

Select the destination account, media, text and visibility before approving a post. Scheduled local jobs require Fusfy to be running. Model and media-generation services retain their own prices and limits.

Fusfy uses YouTube API Services. YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy apply to that connection.
