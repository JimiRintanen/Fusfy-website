Social workspace

Prepare. Review. Publish.

The local publisher sends approved content from your computer to the destination service. It does not require a publishing SaaS subscription.

1. Connect an account

Open Social → Channels in Fusfy and choose Connect. Where configured, authorization opens in your normal browser. The platform shows the requested permissions, and Fusfy verifies the returned account. An application still awaiting provider approval cannot be used as a general public connection.

2. Prepare your content

Create a draft and choose media from your Library. Select a connected destination and its supported settings. For YouTube, the local uploader accepts a video, title, description, visibility and made-for-children setting. The default visibility is private.

3. Review the exact destination and content

The review screen shows what will be sent. A person approves the publication in Fusfy; an agent cannot create its own approval. Model-generated suggestions can be edited before submission.

4. Check the result

The queue distinguishes submitted, processing, published, failed and uncertain outcomes. A TikTok upload still processing is not reported as a finished post. Check the destination service before retrying an uncertain submission.

Scheduling and your computer

Local scheduling requires Fusfy to remain open. Jobs missed by more than fifteen minutes require another review. Interrupted submissions are not automatically repeated. The local engine does not make AI model calls; text, image and video generation are separate features.

Disconnect and revoke access

Disconnect the account in Fusfy's Channels view to remove its locally stored publishing token and cancel queued jobs for that connection. This does not delete previously published content. You can also revoke Google access in Google Account permissions. See the privacy policy for retained local data and backup handling.
